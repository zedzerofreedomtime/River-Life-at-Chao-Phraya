# Unicorn Cruise photography used by River Life

The River Life organiser stated on 2026-10-19 that Unicorn Cruise's sales representative supplied imagery and authorised River Life to use additional photos from the official Facebook page. This records the user's authorisation; it is not an independently obtained licence document. Operator contact details in the photographed hull lettering belong to the vessel and are not River Life customer-service links.
