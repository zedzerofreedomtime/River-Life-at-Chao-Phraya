# Cinematic UI redesign

## Design system
Midnight #080e19, slate #121e2c, champagne #d9b777, white #f5f6f8, muted #b3c0d0, borders #293849. DM Sans with Kanit Thai fallback; DM Sans has no Thai glyphs. Native UI controls, 8px media and panel radii, open editorial layouts, restrained gold separators, responsive 20/48px gutters.

Hero copy: Symphony of the Chao Phraya / A River of Culture. A Night of Music. Explore the experience / Cruises & Booking, localized to Thai. No decorative hero eyebrow. Native video playback controls, reduced-motion and save-data handling, offscreen pause.

## Actual media
The silent 30-second opening is extracted from the supplied UNICORN dinner film: boat 5–10s, Wat Arun 94–98s, buffet 32–37s, seafood 48–52s, Hanuman 111–115s, audience 119–123s, bridge 99–103s. Performance clip 106–125s retains original audio. Full film remains accessible with native sound controls. No generated boat or performance images are published.

## Scope and concept deviations
Homepage sequence: opening film, facts, signature experience, video library, river landmarks, dining, boat story/decks, sailing calendar, booking guide, group contact. Other-boat atmosphere photos are omitted from the sales narrative; originals are retained.

Booking, payment, account, tickets, help and staff inherit the same tokens, typography, forms and navigation. Existing actual zone definitions, booking workflow and role access remain authoritative. Generated concepts are visual hierarchy references, not sources of new features, prices, dates, people, boat imagery or claims. Reject the generated Thames wallet image; use the corrected Bangkok concept. Retain demo warnings, actual QR tokens and black-on-white QR contrast. No new provider or payment behavior.
